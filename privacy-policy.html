Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Thank you for choosing My Talking Monkey. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe mobile experience. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information within the My Talking Monkey application for mobile phones and tablets.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It
My Talking Monkey does not require any user account registration, and we do not collect, store, or share any personal identity information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or location).

However, to keep our application free, we integrate third-party advertising services that may collect limited technical data:

- Network Permissions (INTERNET): The app requires internet access solely to fetch and display banner ads and rewarded ads from Google AdMob.

4. Children’s Privacy
Our application does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since we use Google AdMob, the ads served are compliant with appropriate family and children safety regulations where applicable.
